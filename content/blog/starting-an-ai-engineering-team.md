Some personal news: I’ve been promoted to **Senior AI Engineer at TaxCloud**! I’m starting a brand new team with a broad mandate to tackle business problems using agentic AI.

This is the direction I’ve wanted to take my career for years! Getting to kick-start a team around it makes this a particularly exciting moment. I remember when ChatGPT 3.5 first launched, approaching four years ago now. I was stunned. Amazed. I couldn't believe a computer could write and respond so dynamically. My interest in programming had never really dovetailed with machine learning, and I knew in that moment I had to alter course. The world had changed forever. I've spent considerable time since then learning about how AI is built, how it works, and how to wield it. Being selected for this team isn't just a fun new title. It's recognition that the work - and the hours - mattered.

## A broader engineering problem

The part that excites me most about this change in role is the change in scope. A large gulf of work exists between noticing that a business process is broken, and building something people can actually depend on. Understanding the problem, connecting the systems, figuring out what an agent should be responsible for, and deciding how to tell whether it’s working are all part of that work.

My diverse background in consulting, frontend, integrations, cloud systems, and data pipelines feels especially relevant here. Beyond my passion for AI, this role will demand my entire breadth of career experience to date. I feel incredibly fortunate to join this amazing team at its opening chapter, and to tackle genuinely interesting problems with state-of-the-art, downright SciFi tools!


## DienerTech gets a fresh coat of paint

It's funny to consider the origins of this website. My first commits in the project date back to early March 2025. Call that 18 months ago. At the time, I felt a pressing need to create a personal portfolio. To create some durable artifact that would declare **Yes, I can code!** *(please hire me)*. AI assisted with the development and planning, but the AI of 18 months ago was not able to generate websites with the mere flick of the wrist as it can now. I manually wrote large swaths of DienerTech, building through fun, exploration, and a nagging fear that I would get laid off from my job and starve to death (I turned out to be half right). 

While the website has grown considerably since that original launch, much has stayed the same. This blog post goes live with a site-wide update, modernizing styles, condensing navigation, and bringing DienerTech forward into the future. 

Unlike those early commits, I didn't write a single line of code to make this change. [Ship of Theseus](/blog/ship-of-theseus) considered this future in March of '25 - we are now all living it. That sure didn't take long. I'm going to circle back on this point, because the changes occurring across the industry are both profound and accelerating, and they deserve their own full post.

## Celebrating DienerTech's first product launch - VectorXR

[VectorXR](/products/vectorxr) has become my first open-source product under DienerTech LLC. I am a bit shocked VR tooling became my first software launch, given the focus on AI experimentation that has consumed my time. VectorXR started as a small experiment - Can AI write OpenXR code? To my surprise, it turned out to be remarkably good at it! VectorXR is approaching **3,000 downloads** and has over **50 GitHub stars**, making it far and away my most successful software project to date! 

The full story of VectorXR deserves its own post, coming soon(TM).

Speaking of, [version 0.17.0](https://github.com/DienerTech/vectorxr/releases) just launched, be sure to update for the latest enhancements to the VR Turbo system!
