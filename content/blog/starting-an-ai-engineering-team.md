Some personal news: I’ve been promoted to **Senior AI Engineer at TaxCloud**. I’m starting a brand new team with a broad mandate to tackle business problems using agentic AI.

This is the direction I’ve wanted to take my career for years. Getting to build a team around it makes this a particularly exciting moment.

## A broader engineering problem

The part that excites me most is the scope. There’s a lot of work between noticing that a business process is broken and building something people can actually depend on. Understanding the problem, connecting the systems, figuring out what an agent should be responsible for, and deciding how to tell whether it’s working are all part of that work.

My background in frontend, integrations, cloud systems, and data pipelines feels especially relevant here. Those systems are where an AI capability has to become useful.

This is the beginning of the team’s story. I’m looking forward to finding out what we can build and what the work teaches us.

## Bringing the website along

Outside of work, [SparkNet](/work/sparknet) has taken me deeper into training, fine-tuning, and evaluating language models. [VectorXR](/products/vectorxr) has become my first open source product under DienerTech LLC. Both belong here, alongside the professional work and the occasional detour into D&D or flight simulation.

I’m also making room for shorter notes like this one. The projects tend to move faster than the blog, and a useful observation shouldn’t have to wait for a full retrospective.

There’s a lot more to write about. For now: a new role, a new team, and a very welcome next chapter.
